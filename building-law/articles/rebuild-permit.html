What Permit Questions Should You Ask First?

Before submitting an ADU application, identify the reviewing city, required forms, objective development standards, utility constraints, fire access considerations, and any special review triggers.

A good research question includes the city, property type, ADU type, target size, and any known constraints. That gives the assistant enough context to return a more useful source-grounded answer.
