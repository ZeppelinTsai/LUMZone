Lot Coverage and Floor Area Basics for ADUs

ADU feasibility may depend on lot coverage, floor area, setbacks, open-space standards, and the relationship between the proposed ADU and the existing primary home.

California state ADU law creates important baseline protections, but cities can still publish local standards that affect design choices. Review both state-level rules and city-specific guidance.
