How Many ADUs Can I Build on One Lot in San Diego?

Last updated: May 2026

San Diego ADU count rules depend on whether the property is a single-dwelling lot, a multiple-dwelling lot, an existing multifamily building, or a project using the bonus ADU program.

Direct Answer

On many single-dwelling lots, San Diego guidance provides pathways for one attached or detached ADU, one conversion ADU from existing or proposed space, and one JADU. The exact combination depends on the site and project design.

Multifamily properties can be more powerful. Existing non-livable spaces may be converted into ADUs, and detached ADUs may be allowed based on the number of existing or proposed units.

Transit Priority Areas (TPA)

Properties located inside San Diego Transit Priority Areas (TPA) may qualify for additional ADU flexibility, including parking-related exemptions and pathways commonly used with ADU density bonus projects.

TPA-related development is frequently discussed alongside Complete Communities Housing Solutions and the ADU Home Density Bonus Program.

Single-Dwelling Lots

  • One single primary dwelling unit.
  • One ADU attached to or detached from the dwelling.
  • One ADU converted from existing or proposed space or an existing accessory structure.
  • One JADU.
This is a feasibility framework, not a guarantee. Size, setbacks, height, FAR, coastal rules, fire access, and utilities still matter.

Multiple-Dwelling Properties

For existing multiple-dwelling structures, San Diego allows ADUs in existing non-livable spaces based on a percentage of existing units, with at least one ADU when the calculation yields less than one. Detached ADUs may also be allowed, subject to limits tied to the number of existing or proposed units.

Bonus ADUs

San Diego's ADU Home Density Bonus Program can allow additional ADUs when qualifying affordable ADUs are provided. This path is more complex and can add affordability agreements, fees, parking, tree, accessibility, and sprinkler requirements.
